Questions & Answers
❓ How does the Team plan work?
After payment, you receive a unique invite link. Share it with your team — each PM registers with their own account and gets immediate access. No manual setup on your end.
❓ What's included in all plans?
All AI tools based on PMBOK 8th Edition (Project Charter, Risk Register, WBS, MoM, Stakeholder Matrix, and more), PMP® Exam Trainer with unlimited questions, and PDF export.
❓ Can I upgrade from Personal to Team later?
Yes. Write to support@pm-master.club and we'll adjust your plan. The difference is calculated pro-rata.
❓ How do I cancel?
Email support@pm-master.club — cancelled within 24 hours. No charges for the next billing period.
❓ Is my data safe?
Yes. Each user's projects are private and visible only to them. Team plan gives access to the tools, not to each other's work.
