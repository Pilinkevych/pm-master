# Everything is open to crawlers. /admin.html is deliberately NOT disallowed
# here: it is served with `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` (see netlify.toml), and a
# crawler has to be able to fetch the page to read that header. Blocking it in
# robots.txt would hide the noindex and let the bare URL show up in results
# anyway, which is the opposite of what it is for.
